About

Analysis that goes deeper than the headlines

Fault Lines is an independent publication covering politics, philosophy, and religion. It was built on a simple premise: the most important questions rarely get the depth they deserve.

The name is deliberate. A fault line is the place where tectonic plates meet — where pressure builds invisibly beneath the surface until it cannot be contained. The most consequential forces in our world operate the same way. They are rarely visible in the headlines. They require digging.

"The unexamined life is not worth living." — Socrates

Fault Lines exists to examine. To go back further than the incident, the headline, the talking point. To ask not just what happened but why this was always going to happen — and what it means.

What we cover

The writing spans politics, philosophy, and religion — sometimes separately, often where they intersect.

Politics

Power, conflict, and the structures that govern our lives. With a particular focus on the Middle East and geopolitics.

Philosophy

The ideas that underpin how we think about justice, power, ethics, and what it means to live well.

Religion

Faith, theology, and the role of religion in shaping politics, identity, and civilisation.

On The Ground

First-hand accounts and political tourism — what the places that make the news actually look and feel like.

Who writes this

Fault Lines is written independently with a commitment to going beyond surface-level analysis. No institutional backing. No editorial agenda. No allegiance to any state or faction.

All content is free to read.
